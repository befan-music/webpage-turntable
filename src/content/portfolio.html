01

Quote Builder

Description

A searchable index of an author's quotations, organized by source work and theme. 155 entries across 24 works from 1934–1972, sorted into 7 themes.

Problem

Quotes from an author's body of work circulate as isolated fragments, detached from the book they came from and impossible to browse by idea.

Validation

Faced the problem myself while reading and wanting to trace recurring ideas back across works.

Solution

A card index where every quotation carries its source work, year and theme tags, plus full-text search, filtering by work, and a daily featured pull.

No marketing efforts done
